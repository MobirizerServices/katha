## 1. The three supply routes

| Route | What it is | Cost / 60 eps | Lead time | Owns | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Commission** (originals) | Katha writes or approves the outline; a partner studio produces to spec; Katha takes full buyout | ₹6–70 lakh (Tier C→A) | 6–12 weeks | Everything, forever | Anything that must be *ours*: tentpoles, the brand titles, anything we want a sequel to |
| **Licence + localise** (dubbed imports) | Finished foreign catalogue, dubbed and adapted for India | ₹1–4 lakh | 2–4 weeks | A term licence only | Volume, genre gaps, and the weekly cadence |
| **Creator partnership** | An existing vertical-drama creator brings a format and an audience; Katha funds and co-owns | ₹3–10 lakh + rev share | 4–8 weeks | Co-owned, Katha-exclusive | Top-of-funnel, cheap experiments, discovering directors |

**Default rule:** commission the ~14 titles you would be embarrassed to lose; licence the rest. The bible's 40:60 split *is* this rule expressed as a slate.

### 1.1 The build-vs-buy test (apply per slot, not per slate)

Buy (licence) when **all** are true: the genre is proven and non-specific to India; no sequel value; you need it live in under four weeks; landed cost < ₹4 lakh. **Commission** if any one fails.

## 2. Partner network

### 2.1 Target shape at launch

| Hub | Studios | Languages | Why this city |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mumbai** | 2–3 | Hindi | Deepest crew pool; TV/OTT casting; highest day rates — use for Tier A/B only |
| **Lucknow / Delhi NCR** | 2 | Hindi | Heartland texture, real havelis and streets, 30–40% cheaper than Mumbai; the *Kaanch Ka Mahal* belt |
| **Hyderabad** | 2 | Telugu, Hindi (Dakhni) | Large freelance crew base; strong vertical-format experience; the cheapest good Tier C in India |
| **Chennai / Madurai** | 2 | Tamil | Tamil casting and locations; Madurai for the *Vetri Vaasal* belt |

**Target: 8–10 studios under MSA by week 8.** Never let one studio hold more than **25%** of in-flight episodes — a single missed delivery must not empty a language.

### 2.2 Where to find them (in priority order)

1. **Vertical-format incumbents.** Production houses already delivering micro-drama for ReelShort/DramaBox/Kuku FM-adjacent apps. They know 9:16, they know 60-second beats, they need no education — and they are the fastest to onboard. Find them through the *crew* credits on those apps, not the platform.
2. **Regional TV serial units.** Daily-soap teams shoot 20+ minutes a day for a living. Their speed and cost discipline are exactly right; their vertical framing and pace need one workshop.
3. **Ad-film and music-video houses.** Best image quality per rupee; weakest at volume. Use for tentpoles and clip-factory assets.
4. **Film-school and theatre networks** (FTII, SRFTI, LV Prasad, Ramanaidu, local theatre groups) for directors and cast on Tier C.
5. **Creator-led studios** — YouTube/Instagram short-drama creators with 100k+ followers who already shoot vertical.

### 2.3 Studio scorecard (score before the MSA; 3 = meets bar)

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vertical 9:16 experience | Never | Some | Ships weekly |
| Delivery reliability (references) | Slips routinely | Mostly on time | Never missed |
| Crew depth (can run 2 units) | No | With notice | Yes |
| Sound quality on location | Post-fixes | Boom + lav | Consistent −16 LUFS masters |
| Casting reach | Local only | Regional TV | Recognisable OTT faces |
| Compliance hygiene (releases, clearances) | Ad hoc | On request | Standard practice |
| Cost per finished minute | High | Market | Below market at the same quality |

**Onboard at ≥ 21/35 with no 1s in delivery or compliance.** Start every new studio on **one Tier C title** before anything bigger, regardless of score.

## 3. Rate cards

### 3.1 Commissioning rate card (per 60-episode series, all-in to delivered master)

| Tier | Band | Shoot days | Cast | Locations | Per-episode | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** tentpole | ₹40–70 L | 18–25 | Lead + known name + 8 | 6–10, incl. one build | ₹67k–1.17L | 10% of slate |
| **B** core original | ₹15–25 L | 8–12 | Lead + 6 | 3–5 | ₹25k–42k | 40% |
| **C** lean original | ₹6–12 L | 3–5 | Lead + 3 | 1–2 | ₹10k–20k | 20% |
| **D** licensed dub | ₹1–4 L | — | Voice cast | — | ₹1.7k–6.7k | 30% |

**Payment gates — never pay ahead of a deliverable:**

| Gate | % | Released on |
|---|---|---|
| Signing | 20% | Executed MSA + work order + insurance certificate |
| Script lock | 15% | All episodes locked, legal read clear |
| Shoot completion | 25% | Wrap report + rushes handed over |
| First cut (E1–E10) | 20% | Passes auto-QC and the cliff test (§3.3 of the bible) |
| Final delivery | 15% | All episodes pass QC; transcripts and releases complete |
| **Holdback** | **5%** | 30 days after publish, no rights or quality claims |

**Overage rule:** no overage is payable without a written change order signed *before* the spend. Two unapproved overages ends the MSA.

### 3.2 Talent rate guidance (per shoot day; validate locally)

| Role | Tier C | Tier B | Tier A |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead (carries the series) | ₹8–15k | ₹20–40k | ₹1–3L |
| Supporting | ₹3–6k | ₹6–12k | ₹15–40k |
| Recognisable TV/OTT name | — | — | ₹3–10L (series deal) |
| Director | ₹10–20k | ₹25–50k | ₹1.5–4L (series deal) |
| DP | ₹8–15k | ₹15–30k | ₹75k–2L (series deal) |

Buyouts, not royalties, at every tier — **plus a discretionary success bonus** on the lead of any title that hits ≥ 3× payback. It costs little, and it is why good actors come back.

### 3.3 Licensing rate guidance (per 60-episode equivalent, India rights)

| Element | Band | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Licence fee (2-year, India, 3 languages) | ₹60k–2.5L | Per title; volume deals of 10+ cut this 30–40% |
| AI dub (3 languages) + mix | ₹25–60k | §22 pipeline; per-language marginal cost falls fast |
| Human dub QC + retakes | ₹15–40k | Non-negotiable; the AI pass is a draft |
| Localisation (names, on-screen text, art) | ₹20–50k | The line most often under-budgeted |
| Cover art + clips + metadata | ₹10–25k | Never reuse the licensor's key art |
| **Landed total** | **₹1.3–4.2L** | Matches the bible's §6 bands |

**Deal shape to push for:** 2-year India term, **all three languages**, marketing-clip rights, an option on years 3–4 at a pre-agreed uplift, and a **most-favoured-nation** clause on any title the licensor later sells to a competitor at a lower rate.

## 4. Term sheets (drafting briefs for counsel)

### 4.1 Master Service Agreement — production partner

```
PARTIES / TERM        Katha <> Studio. 24 months, auto-renew 12,
                      terminable for cause on 15 days' notice.
STRUCTURE             MSA sets standing terms; each series is a
                      WORK ORDER (scope, tier, episodes, fee,
                      schedule, cast, locations).
RIGHTS                Full buyout, work-for-hire. All media, all
                      territories, all languages, in perpetuity.
                      Includes: dubbing and subtitling; SYNTHETIC
                      VOICE and AI dubbing; marketing clips and
                      cut-downs; sequel, prequel, spin-off and
                      remake rights; format rights.
CHAIN OF TITLE        Studio warrants it has secured identical
                      buyouts from every writer, director, DP,
                      composer, editor and performer, and delivers
                      signed copies as a delivery item. No delivery
                      item, no final payment.
DELIVERY SPEC         PDD 7.2, verbatim, as a schedule.
SLA                   Tier B: 6 weeks concept-to-master.
                      Tier C: 4 weeks. Late delivery: 2% of the
                      series fee per week, capped at 10%.
QUALITY               Two-strike clause: two titles failing QC or
                      the scorecard floor ends the MSA at Katha's
                      option, with in-flight work delivered.
EXCLUSIVITY           Studio may not produce vertical micro-drama
                      for a NAMED competitor list for the term +
                      6 months. Not a general non-compete.
CONFIDENTIALITY       Outlines, scripts, scorecards, rates.
COMPLIANCE            Studio warrants: no infringing material; all
                      permissions and location releases held; no
                      minors employed contrary to law; POSH
                      committee in place and a safe set; insurance
                      maintained.
INSURANCE             Studio carries public liability and workers'
                      cover. Katha carries E&O across all originals.
INDEMNITY             Studio indemnifies for IP, likeness, defamation
                      and employment claims arising from its work.
                      Cap: the aggregate fees paid, EXCEPT uncapped
                      for wilful IP infringement or a POSH claim.
PAYMENT               Gates per 3.1. Net 15 days on each gate.
CREDITS               Katha owns and controls all credits and
                      billing. Studio gets a production credit.
LAW / VENUE           Indian law; seat as advised by counsel;
                      arbitration under the Arbitration and
                      Conciliation Act, one arbitrator.
```

### 4.2 Licence agreement — dubbed imports

```
GRANT       Non-exclusive-in-form, EXCLUSIVE-IN-INDIA for the term,
            for streaming on Katha-controlled surfaces.
TERRITORY   India (+ named diaspora storefronts if priced).
LANGUAGES   Hindi, Tamil, Telugu (+ options on Bengali, Marathi,
            Kannada, Malayalam at a pre-agreed rate).
TERM        24 months + option on 24 more at <= 110% of the fee.
RIGHTS      Dub (human AND synthetic), subtitle, re-title, re-cut
            for episode length, replace on-screen text, create
            NEW key art and marketing clips.
DELIVERY    Masters at or above PDD 7.2, or the licensor funds a
            conform. Script and continuity in the source language.
            Music cue sheet - REQUIRED, no cue sheet no deal.
WARRANTIES  Licensor owns or controls all elements INCLUDING music
            and performer rights for dubbing and synthetic voice,
            and holds the exporter-country clearances.
CONTENT     Katha may edit for Indian legal, IT Rules 2021 and
            platform-policy compliance without approval.
TAKEDOWN    Katha may pull a title within 24 hours on a legal or
            regulatory notice, with a pro-rata fee refund.
MFN         If the licensor licenses the same title in India at a
            lower effective rate within the term, Katha's rate
            reduces to match.
PAYMENT     40% on execution, 40% on accepted delivery, 20% on
            30 days live with no claims. Withholding tax and any
            equalisation levy handled per counsel/CA advice.
```

### 4.3 Performer appearance & AI-voice release (one per person on screen or mic)

```
I, [NAME], [AGE 18+], of [ADDRESS], confirm:

1. GRANT. I irrevocably permit Katha [entity] and its licensees
   to record, use and exploit my performance, name, image,
   likeness, voice and biographical details in [SERIES] and in
   marketing for it, in all media and all territories, forever.

2. DUBBING AND SYNTHETIC VOICE. I specifically permit my voice
   and performance to be dubbed, subtitled, and reproduced using
   AI or synthetic-voice technology, FOR THIS SERIES AND ITS
   MARKETING ONLY. Katha will not use a synthetic model of my
   voice for any other production, or license it to a third
   party, without a separate signed agreement and a separate fee.

3. FEE. [AMOUNT], receipt acknowledged, is the full and final
   consideration. No royalty or residual is payable.

4. NO OBLIGATION. Katha need not use the material.

5. EDITING. I accept that my performance may be edited, dubbed,
   subtitled and re-cut, and waive moral-rights claims to the
   extent permitted by law - EXCEPT that Katha will not depict
   me in a manner that is defamatory, obscene, or endorses a
   product, political party or religious position.

6. WARRANTY. My participation breaches no other agreement.

7. LAW. Indian law.

Signature / Date / Witness / Photo ID reference
```

Notes for counsel: clause 2's narrow synthetic-voice grant is deliberate — a broad AI-voice buyout is the clause performers' bodies push back on hardest, and a title-scoped grant is both defensible and enough for §22's dubbing pipeline. **Minors:** never use this form; a separate guardian consent plus statutory working-hour compliance is required.

### 4.4 Location release (short form)

Owner grants access on named dates; permits filming of the interior/exterior and its use in all media forever; confirms authority to grant; no compensation beyond the agreed fee; Katha restores any alteration and carries public-liability cover. **Get it in writing before the recce, not on the shoot morning.**

## 5. Greenlight rubric (PDD §23.4 Concept gate, made scorable)

Score each 1–5. **Advance at ≥ 24/40 with no 1 in Compliance or Cost.**

| # | Criterion | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook strength** — is the E1 turn legible in one sentence? | Needs a paragraph | One sentence, and you want E2 |
| 2 | **Trope demand** — does the scorecard show this trope converting? | No data, no comp | ≥ 2 comps above the cliff floor |
| 3 | **Cliff design** — is the E10 turn already known and of type 5/10/12? | "We'll find it" | Written, and it pays off *and* opens |
| 4 | **Specificity** — could this be set anywhere? | Generic city | A place with food, transport and dialect |
| 5 | **Castability** — can we cast the lead in 3 weeks at tier rate? | One name, unavailable | Three real options |
| 6 | **Cost fit** — does it shoot at its tier? | Needs a crowd, a build, or a night exterior every episode | 1–2 locations, cast of six |
| 7 | **Compliance** | Caste/religion/real-person exposure | Clean; U/A 13+ or lower |
| 8 | **Franchise** — is there a season 2 or a spin-off in it? | Closed | An open door already written |

**Kill fast.** A concept scoring 18–23 is not "developed further" — it is rewritten from the hook or dropped. The scarce resource is shoot weeks, not ideas.

## 6. From delivery to live (the operational chain)

| # | Step | Owner | SLA | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Master + transcript + releases delivered to the ingest bucket | Studio | Per work order | Delivery checklist §6.1 complete |
| 2 | Auto-QC probe against §7.2 | Pipeline | Same day | Hard fail → back to studio, no payment |
| 3 | Human spot-QC: E1, E10, E11, plus 10% random | Content Ops | 48 h | The cliff test and the train test |
| 4 | AI subtitles + dub | AI platform (§22) | ≤ 2 h | — |
| 5 | Human dub/subtitle QC | Language QC | 48 h | Name and idiom accuracy |
| 6 | Rating + descriptors (IT Rules 2021 Part III) | Content Ops | With step 3 | Recorded in admin |
| 7 | Cover art (9:16 + 16:9), trailer ≤ 45 s, cliffhanger frames | Design | 72 h | Never the licensor's key art |
| 8 | Schedule + publish | Programming | Weekly meeting | Four weeks of calendar visible |
| 9 | Clip factory: 3–5 cuts for paid and organic | Growth (§24.2) | ≤ 24 h from publish | — |
| 10 | Scorecard review | Content + Data | Day 14, Day 90 | Renew / sequel / shelve |

### 6.1 Delivery checklist (a title is not delivered until every box is ticked)

- [ ] Every episode: 1080×1920, one consistent frame rate, ≥ 8 Mbps H.264 / ≥ 5 Mbps HEVC
- [ ] Audio stereo AAC ≥ 192 kbps, **−16 LUFS ± 1**, no clipping, dialogue intelligible on a phone speaker
- [ ] Safe zones respected: bottom 22%, right 12% clear of faces and text
- [ ] Every episode ends on a hook; hook type labelled in the delivered beat sheet
- [ ] E10 runs 5–8 s long and ships a chosen cliffhanger still
- [ ] Source-language transcript per episode, timecoded
- [ ] Signed appearance/AI-voice release per performer, with ID reference
- [ ] Signed location releases; property releases for any distinctive artwork in frame
- [ ] Music cue sheet with licence evidence, or original score work-for-hire papers
- [ ] Chain-of-title pack: writer, director, DP, composer, editor buyouts
- [ ] No brands, logos, real-person likenesses, or uniform insignia in frame
- [ ] Rating recommendation + descriptors from the studio (Katha decides)

## 7. Outreach templates

### 7.1 Production studio — first contact

> **Subject: Commissioning vertical micro-drama in [Hindi/Tamil/Telugu] — 6–12 series this quarter**
>
> Hello [Name],
>
> I'm [You], Head of Content at Katha — a new India-first vertical drama app launching this quarter in Hindi, Tamil and Telugu. We're commissioning **6–12 series per quarter** at 45–70 episodes of 60–90 seconds each, and we're building a small panel of studios in [city] to make them.
>
> Concretely, one of our first titles is a **[Tier C, single-location, 48-episode Hindi thriller]** — roughly [3–5] shoot days, a cast of four, and a fixed delivery spec we'd share up front. We commission on **full buyout**, pay against **six delivery gates** (20% at signing), and settle **net 15**.
>
> If that's a shape you shoot, could we do 30 minutes this week? I'd want to see one vertical piece you've delivered and hear what a 48-episode order does to your schedule.
>
> [You] · [phone] · [email]

**Why it works:** a real order, a real tier, a real payment term, and a small ask. Do not open with a deck.

### 7.2 Licensor / distributor — catalogue enquiry

> **Subject: India licence — 10–16 titles, Hindi/Tamil/Telugu dubbing rights**
>
> Hello [Name],
>
> Katha is an India-first vertical micro-drama service launching this quarter. We're acquiring **10–16 completed series** for India, and I'd like to see what you have in **romance, family drama and comedy**, 45–70 episodes.
>
> The rights we need: **India, 24 months, exclusive in India**, with **Hindi, Tamil and Telugu dubbing (human and synthetic), re-titling, on-screen-text replacement, new key art, and marketing clips**. We deliver dubs ourselves.
>
> Could you send an availability list with episode counts, source language, and whether **music cue sheets and performer dubbing consents** are in place? Cue sheets are a gate for us. If the list works we can move to a volume deal across 10+ titles.
>
> [You]

### 7.3 Creator partnership

> **Subject: Funding your next vertical series — Katha**
>
> [Name] — your [series/reel] is doing the thing our whole format is about: a turn every sixty seconds. We're Katha, launching in [language] this quarter.
>
> The offer: **we fund a 45-episode series** you direct, at ₹[3–10] lakh, you keep a co-owner's share and a success bonus, and it goes out exclusively on Katha with your name on it. We handle post, subtitles, dubs and marketing.
>
> One 30-minute call, and if you're in, I'll send a one-page outline brief. Nothing exclusive until we both sign.

## 8. The 16-week sourcing timeline (runs against PDD §16)

| Week | Content ops | Legal / finance |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Head of Content in seat; longlist 25 studios and 6 licensors; publish the bible and this playbook | Counsel briefed on MSA, licence, releases; E&O quotes |
| 3–4 | Studio meetings and scorecards; shoot the **Kaanch Ka Mahal** pilot | MSA template final; entity and PAN/GST ready for vendor onboarding |
| 5–6 | Sign the first **4 MSAs**; work orders for 4 Tier C originals; licensor availability lists in | Licence template final; first 2 licences signed |
| 7–8 | Sign remaining MSAs (8–10 total); Tier A tentpoles into casting; **8 acquisitions contracted** | Insurance bound; chain-of-title pack in the delivery checklist |
| 9–10 | First Tier C masters land; auto-QC and the dub pipeline get their real test | Payment gates running; first holdbacks tracked |
| 11–12 | Tier B originals shooting; pilot tests (E1–E5) with 200–500 beta users | Ratings/descriptors process signed off by counsel |
| 13–14 | Tentpole delivery; cover art, trailers, clip factory; **bench: 6 finished series per language** | Grievance officer appointed (this is a beta blocker) |
| 15 | Full slate ingested, rated, scheduled; drop calendar four weeks out | Final compliance sweep across all 30 titles |
| 16 | **Soft launch.** 30 titles live | Day-14 scorecard review scheduled |

**The critical path is not production — it is the MSA.** Nothing shoots before counsel finishes the buyout and the AI-voice release. Start week 1.

## 9. Risks specific to sourcing

| Risk | Signal | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| One studio holds too much of a language | > 25% of in-flight episodes | Cap at MSA level; keep a reserve studio warm in each hub |
| Chain of title incomplete at delivery | Missing writer or composer buyouts | Make the pack a **payment gate**, not a follow-up |
| Licensor can't clear music | No cue sheet | No cue sheet, no deal — walk |
| AI-dub quality below the "quality bar" pillar | Human QC retake rate > 20% | Human QC is mandatory; budget it, never trim it |
| Dubbed imports carry an unshippable premise | Localisation quotes exceed the licence | Apply §6's must-not-have list at longlist, not at delivery |
| Talent consent challenged on synthetic voice | Performer or union query | Title-scoped grant (§4.3 cl. 2) + separate fee for reuse |
| Cost per episode drifts above tier | Overages requested mid-shoot | Change orders in writing, before spend; two strikes |
| Launch slate slips | Bench < 6 per language at week 13 | Convert two Tier C commissions to acquisitions — the pressure valve is bought volume |
