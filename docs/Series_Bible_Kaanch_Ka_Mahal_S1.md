# Kaanch Ka Mahal — काँच का महल
## Season 1 Series Bible · 60 episodes · Hindi · Family Drama / Revenge

*Authored 6 September 2026. Written to be shot by the Katha AI pipeline.*

---

## 0. Why the first attempt failed

The generated pilot was 56 seconds of atmosphere. It is worth naming the faults precisely, because every rule in this bible exists to prevent one of them.

**Meera never acted.** She arrived, she sat, she opened an album, she looked. A protagonist who only receives information is a camera, not a character. In this bible Meera *wants* something on page one and takes an illegal action to get it inside the first eight seconds.

**One line of dialogue in a whole episode.** Micro-drama is an argument format. People lie to each other's faces and the audience watches them do it. Silence is a spice, not a meal.

**No clock, no stakes, no cost.** Nothing bad happened if Meera did nothing. Every episode below has a thing she can lose.

**The reveal was the ending, not the engine.** "A man walks in" is a fine button but the story had nowhere to go afterward. The structure below produces a new reversal roughly every ten episodes and keeps two secrets running against each other for the whole season.

---

## 1. The format spec

Research on the vertical format is unambiguous and I have written to it rather than to taste:

- **Episode length: 75–90 seconds.** Long enough for three real beats, short enough to demand the next one.
- **The skeleton is Hook / Friction / Spike / Button**, drafted as timestamps before a word of dialogue is written.
- **The button is the product.** It is not an ending, it is the structural destination. It lands in the final 3–5 seconds and freezes.
- **No small talk.** In ninety seconds, a line that does not move the scene is a line that does not exist. No greetings, no "how was your day", no exposition delivered as conversation between people who both already know the facts.
- **The hook is understandable in seconds.** *The new bride is the dead bride's sister.* That is the whole show, and it is legible instantly.

**Per-episode timing target (85s):**

| Time | Beat | Function |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | **Hook** | Land in motion. Never establish, never arrive. |
| 0:08–0:35 | **Friction** | Two people want opposite things and say so. |
| 0:35–1:05 | **Spike** | The scene turns. New information, publicly. |
| 1:05–1:20 | **Button** | The reversal, then freeze. |

## 2. Logline

Two years after her elder sister died as a bride in the Rathore haveli, **Meera enters that house under a false name as the new bride** — to prove it was murder. She has forty days until the family's Sharad puja settles the estate and buries the last of the evidence.

**Tagline:** *Kabir has no brother.*

---

## 3. The cast

**MEERA (27)** — the lead. Not a victim and not a detective: a liar with a deadline. Warm and correct on the surface because she has to be; underneath, calculating every conversation. Her flaw is that she is *not actually cold* — she keeps forming real attachments to the people she is deceiving, and that is what will nearly destroy her. She married a man she intends to expose.

**SUSHILA (58)** — the matriarch. Runs the haveli by ritual and refusal. Reads as the villain for thirty episodes and is not one. Her cruelty is the cruelty of someone protecting a secret so heavy that kindness has become a luxury she cannot afford. The season's best reversal belongs to her.

**KABIR (34)** — the husband. Married Meera because his mother arranged it and because he is tired. He knows more than he says and less than she assumes. Genuinely decent, comprehensively compromised. The audience must want Meera to trust him and must never be sure she should.

**ARUN (34)** — the man in the photograph. The elder son the family has erased from the record. Charming, unhurried, faintly amused, and entirely unreliable as a narrator of his own story. Ally and threat in the same breath.

**DEVENDRA (61)** — the family lawyer and Sushila's late husband's brother. Smiles constantly. Holds the estate, the paperwork, and everyone's throat. The real architect, revealed in stages.

**NISHA (30)** — Meera's elder sister. Kabir's first wife. Declared dead two years ago. She is not dead.

**KAMLA (50s)** — house help, dismissed in episode 11 for knowing too much.

**THE GARDENER (70s)** — mute, was Nisha's only friend, can write. The season's final key.

## 4. The truth, in the order the audience learns it

This is the spine. Each layer recontextualises everything before it.

1. **Episodes 1–15 — Arun exists.** A man stands beside Kabir in every wedding photograph and the family denies he is real. He is the elder son, erased from the family record after 2023.
2. **Episodes 16–29 — Nisha did not die the way they said.** Her diary surfaces. She was pregnant. A birth is registered; no death is. The child is alive and inside the house.
3. **Episode 30 — MIDPOINT: Nisha is alive.** Held in the north wing, declared dead on paper.
4. **Episodes 31–45 — Kabir knew.** The doctor who signed the certificate signed what he was told, and so did the magistrate. This is bigger than a family.
5. **Episodes 46–50 — REVERSAL: Sushila is not the villain.** She hid Nisha to keep her alive. The torn diary page names Devendra.
6. **Episodes 51–60 — Devendra built all of it** to hold the estate through a fabricated death and a forged guardianship. The glass house where Nisha's "accident" happened holds the proof.

## 5. Season map

### Block 1 · Episodes 1–10 — The man who doesn't exist

**1. "Dobara"** — Meera tears a photograph out of the wedding album and hides it as footsteps approach. Sushila welcomes her with a warning about everything having its place. Meera produces the photo and asks who the man is. *"There is no such man in this house."* The doorbell. He walks in: *"Bhabhi? Nobody told me Kabir had married again."* — **BUTTON: "Again?"** Sushila, hard: *"Kabir has no brother."*

**2. "Nisha"** — Meera demands to know what "again" meant. Kabir admits a first wife who died. Meera must hear her own sister's death described to her as news, and hold her face still. **BUTTON:** Kabir says the name — *Nisha* — and Sushila is watching Meera's hands, not her face.

**3. "The torn corners"** — Arun is housed as a guest, not blood. Meera corners him. *"Ask them why there are no photographs of me after 2023."* **BUTTON:** every album photo after that date has a corner torn away.

**4. "Kheer"** — Sushila tests Meera with a ritual dish only a Rathore bahu is taught. Meera passes — because Nisha taught her. **BUTTON:** *"Nisha made it exactly the same way. Strange."*

**5. "The locked room"** — Nisha's old room is repainted and locked. Meera gets in. **BUTTON:** her sister's hairpin, under a floorboard.

**6. "Go and look at the date"** — Arun tells her to leave the house tonight. *"The last woman who asked about me is in the family cemetery. Go and look at the date."*  **BUTTON:** Meera goes.

**7. "One month early"** — The date carved on the grave is a month *before* the date on the death certificate the family produced. **BUTTON:** Kabir is standing behind her.

**8. "Di"** — Kabir asks why his new wife is at his first wife's grave on day four. Meera lies well. **BUTTON:** *"You called her Nisha di."*

**9. "Forty days"** — Meera covers the slip. Sushila announces the Sharad puja in forty days, when the estate will be settled. The clock starts. **BUTTON:** Arun: *"That's the day they finish what they started."*

**10. "If you're reading this"** — Meera finds Nisha's diary bricked into the temple room. First line: *"If you are reading this, I am already gone."* **BUTTON:** the final entry names the person responsible — and that page has been torn out.

### Block 2 · Episodes 11–20 — The diary

**11.** Meera hunts the missing page. Kamla flinches at the right question. **BUTTON:** Kamla is dismissed from service that night.
**12.** Meera catches Kamla at the bus stand. *"Don't trust the man who says he is the brother."* **BUTTON:** Arun is standing three feet away.
**13.** Arun's account: he was cut out for objecting to how Nisha was treated. **BUTTON:** he produces a photograph of himself with Nisha — visibly pregnant.
**14.** Meera never knew her sister was pregnant. Kabir says there was no child. **BUTTON:** Sushila, alone with her: *"The child is not your concern."*
**15.** Meera has a friend pull hospital records. **BUTTON:** a girl was born. No death is registered for her.
**16.** Meera pushes Kabir hard and he breaks. **BUTTON:** *"If you keep asking, they will do to you what they did to her."*
**17.** First fragile alliance with Kabir. **BUTTON:** she returns to her room; the diary is gone.
**18.** Meera feeds three different lies to three people to find the informer. **BUTTON:** the lie that travels is the one she told Devendra's man.
**19.** Devendra arrives properly — all warmth, no heat. **BUTTON:** *"Your sister had the same eyes."*
**20.** He knows exactly who she is and has told no one. **BUTTON:** *"I'm not going to expose you. I'm going to use you."*

### Block 3 · Episodes 21–30 — The price

**21.** Devendra's offer: the truth, in exchange for her signature at the puja. Meera refuses.
**22.** Sushila confiscates her phone. **BUTTON:** a second diary page appears under her door. Someone inside is helping.
**23.** Meera hunts the helper through Arun, Kabir and the servants. **BUTTON:** it is the mute gardener.
**24.** He leads her to a padlocked outhouse. **BUTTON:** children's toys inside, and they are not two years old.
**25.** A child cries in the house at night. **BUTTON:** Sushila in the corridor: *"This house makes sounds. Don't follow them."*
**26.** Meera follows them. **BUTTON:** an empty room, and the bed is still warm.
**27.** Arun and Meera nearly reach the north wing. **BUTTON:** Kabir bolts the door from the other side — *"Not tonight. Trust me once."*
**28.** Kabir confesses the child is his daughter, hidden after Sushila had Nisha declared unfit. **BUTTON:** *"And Nisha—"* Sushila enters.
**29.** Meera forces the end of the sentence out of him. **BUTTON:** *"Nisha didn't die."*
**30. MIDPOINT — "The north wing"** — Meera crosses the courtyard at night and sees her sister's face at a barred window. **BUTTON:** Nisha looks straight at her and shows no recognition at all.

### Block 4 · Episodes 31–40 — The north wing

**31.** The wing is sealed. **BUTTON:** the key hangs on a chain around Sushila's neck, never removed.
**32.** Meera plays the perfect daughter-in-law to get close enough to take it. Sushila embraces her. **BUTTON:** *"I know who you are."*
**33.** Sushila's counter: she announces to the family that Meera is pregnant — a lie that locks her in the house. **BUTTON:** a doctor is called to confirm it.
**34.** Meera evades the examination. **BUTTON:** the doctor is the man who signed Nisha's death certificate.
**35.** Meera corners him. **BUTTON:** *"I signed what I was told to sign. So did the magistrate."*
**36.** The conspiracy is larger than the family. **BUTTON:** Devendra: *"Now you understand my offer."*
**37.** Meera takes the deal to get the key. **BUTTON:** what she signs is a transfer of the child's guardianship.
**38.** She realises what she has signed. **BUTTON:** *"Your sister signed the same page. That is why she stopped being useful."*
**39.** Meera opens the north wing. **BUTTON:** the room is empty. Nisha has been moved.
**40. DARKEST TURN — "Out"** — Devendra exposes Meera's identity to the whole family at dinner. Sushila: *"Get her out of my house."* Kabir says nothing. **BUTTON:** the gate closes behind her at night, with nothing in her hands.

### Block 5 · Episodes 41–50 — The outsider

**41.** No phone, no proof, no name. **BUTTON:** Arun's car pulls up. *"Now we do it my way."*
**42.** Arun's way is the first marriage certificate proving he is the elder son. **BUTTON:** it is in the temple safe, inside the house she was just thrown out of.
**43.** They plan re-entry through the puja preparations. **BUTTON:** Kabir finds them — and asks to come.
**44.** Three-way alliance. **BUTTON:** Kabir carries his daughter out of the haveli.
**45.** Sushila finds the child gone. **BUTTON:** *"Then we do it the old way."* She calls Devendra.
**46.** Devendra moves to have Nisha declared legally dead before the puja. **BUTTON:** the hearing is in three days.
**47.** Meera finds Nisha in a private facility under a false name. **BUTTON:** Nisha does not know her.
**48.** Two years of sedation. **BUTTON:** ninety seconds of lucidity — *"Meera. The page. Devendra."*
**49.** The torn page named Devendra all along, never Sushila. **BUTTON:** which means Sushila was hiding Nisha *from* him.
**50. REVERSAL — "I kept her alive"** — Sushila finds Meera. **BUTTON:** *"I kept her alive for two years. You have had her for two days and you have killed her."*

### Block 6 · Episodes 51–60 — The glass breaks

**51.** Sushila's account: Devendra's legal chokehold, and why silence was the only protection she had.
**52.** Uneasy alliance with the woman she came to destroy. **BUTTON:** Devendra brings the puja forward to tomorrow.
**53.** A race to get Nisha to the hearing. **BUTTON:** she is moved again in the night.
**54.** Arun offers to renounce his claim in exchange for Nisha. **BUTTON:** Devendra agrees far too easily.
**55.** It was a trap; Arun is taken. **BUTTON:** Devendra holds the guardianship paper with Meera's signature on it.
**56.** Meera's counter is the birth record plus an eyewitness: the gardener. **BUTTON:** he cannot speak.
**57.** He can write. **BUTTON:** he writes one word, and it is not a name — it is a place.
**58.** The old glass house where Nisha's accident happened. **BUTTON:** Devendra is already standing in it.
**59.** The confrontation. The glass goes. **BUTTON:** Nisha walks in, lucid, and says his name.
**60. "The photograph"** — Devendra broken, Nisha restored to her daughter, Arun back in the record, Meera staying because she chooses to. **BUTTON:** a new family photograph — everyone in frame, no one torn out. *"Ab is ghar mein koi mitaya nahin jayega."*

## 7. Production rules for the pipeline

1. **Cast once, shoot forever.** One reference still per character, generated first; every shot's key frame is conditioned on it. This is what held Meera's face together across the pilot and it is non-negotiable.
2. **Lip-sync every on-camera line.** Silent video plus a voice track over the top is what made the pilot feel like a slideshow. Dialogue shots go through `fal-ai/latentsync` after animation.
3. **Faces for dialogue.** A speaking shot is framed medium or closer, front-on enough for a lip-sync model to find the mouth.
4. **Ambience per shot, never silence.** Room tone under everything, generated from the shot's own audio note.
5. **The button freezes.** The final shot holds on a face for a full beat before cutting to the end card.
