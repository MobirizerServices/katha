Questions people ask

Can't find it here? The in-app assistant answers in Hindi and English, and a person steps in when needed.

How do coins work?

The first 10 episodes of every series are free. After that each episode costs 30 coins, about ₹4.5. You buy coins in packs through the App Store; they never expire while your account exists, and bonus coins are spent before purchased coins.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases made through the App Store are refunded by Apple under Apple's policy — you can request one from your Apple account. If you paid but didn't receive coins, contact us and we'll fix it, usually within hours.

Which languages are available?

Hindi, Tamil and Telugu at launch. Bengali, Marathi, Kannada and Malayalam follow. Most series are available in all three launch languages with English subtitles; the app and this site are in English and Hindi, with Tamil and Telugu on the way.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone running iOS 17 or later. You can also watch the free episodes of any series on this website. An Android app is planned.

Is there a parental lock?

Yes. Set a 4-digit PIN in Settings and it will be required before any series rated U/A 16+ or A plays. Katha is for viewers 18 and older.

Can I watch offline?

Not yet. Downloads are on the roadmap. Data saver keeps mobile-data usage low in the meantime.
