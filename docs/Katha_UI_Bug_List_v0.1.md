# Katha — UI/UX layout bug list v0.1 (4 September 2026)

Screenshot-backed audit of every screen and state on three surfaces. Severity: **P1** blocks a flow · **P2** visibly wrong · **P3** polish.
Screenshots live under the session scratchpad (`…/scratchpad/uiaudit/<surface>/`); the
evidence column names the file.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Web app + marketing site | 2 | 13 | 29 | 44 |
| Admin panel | 5 | 15 | 15 | 35 |
| iOS app | 0 | 6 | 23 | 29 |

## Fix-first (P1 across surfaces)

- **WEB-01** Player seek bar and timestamp are fake (fixed 38 % fill, hard-coded `0:23 / 1:04`), no play/pause or seek control.
- **WEB-02** Content rating and the parental gate use the static seed rating, not the server's; a 16+ title plays without a PIN when the panel has re-rated it.
- **ADM-01** Bare `<label><input>` controls in almost every modal and the Writers' Room render as unstyled white browser inputs that truncate typed text.
- **ADM-02** Feature-flag switches have no on/off styling, so operators cannot see which flags are on.
- **ADM-03** The approvals inbox, sidebar badge and Finance count do not refresh after a >500 request until a hard reload.
- **ADM-04** The user ledger table overflows its 520 px modal; the Refund button is out of view with no scrollbar.
- **ADM-05** Media & QC nests the episode table inside the series row, overflowing the page at 1024 px.

iOS has no P1; its top P2s are **IOS-01** (tab bar stays over the full-screen player), **IOS-02** (Dynamic Type ignored on every custom screen) and **IOS-03** (Hindi app language covers only tabs, Settings and the paywall).
